# Listing Seller Advisor Prompts V0

Static, read-only templates for preparing eBay listing guidance.

Core principle:

Readiness first, creativity after.

These prompts do not publish listings, create drafts, call eBay APIs, pause listings, change inventory, or perform any account action. They are planning templates only and require human approval before any operational step.

## Flow

1. Listing readiness review.
2. Seller strategy decision.
3. Title optimization.
4. Image conversion plan.
5. Description conversion.
6. Launch observation plan.
7. Final seller conclusion.

## Readiness Gate

Block or warn before creative work if any required input is missing:

- Confirmed stock.
- Weight or dimensions.
- Authorized images.
- Category and item specifics.
- Sufficient margin.
- Reliable shipping.
- Viable supplier.
- Pack strategy when applicable.
- Low-stock or cancellation risk review.

## Guardrails

- Human approval is required.
- Do not invent data.
- Do not include medical claims.
- Do not include unverified compatibility.
- Do not use unauthorized brands.
- Do not instruct automatic publication.
- Do not create real drafts.
- Do not call eBay APIs.
- Do not enable campaigns until observation supports it.
